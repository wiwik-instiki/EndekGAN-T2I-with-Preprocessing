# Public/private experiment release policy

This public repository contains source-count summaries, non-private experimental code, numerical evaluation results and charts reconstructed from numeric tables. Full training logs, private textile photographs, derived source crops, raw prompts, source-group manifests, Kaggle archives, private provenance and heavyweight checkpoints stay on the authorized local research computer.

- No newly uploaded original or cropped Endek photographs in the 20 November 2026 release. Selected manuscript Figure 1 montage must receive redistribution-rights verification before further use.
- TiPGAN is trained from a single **training** source image per category and is image-conditioned; it has not been evaluated as a text-to-image model.
- Internal Endek T2I and adapted DF-GAN held-out summaries use 37 independent real references: FID/KID remain exploratory and statistical significance and cultural authenticity have not been established.
- A successful checkpoint transfer or train-only stopping signal is not a model-quality benchmark.
- Manuscript figures are distinct from supplementary comparison chart IDs.

For the manuscript-aligned public artifact inventory and precise restrictions, see [Code and Data Availability](CODE_AND_DATA_AVAILABILITY_IEEE.md). The repository already displays a selected source-image montage in Figure 1; redistribution rights for those examples still require verification. No full source-image dataset has been published.
